# Selection before held-out inference

The release uses the direct route by default on this target Mac. The trial on three development pages produced 3/8 exact fields with hybrid and 1/8 with direct. Of seven answerable fields, hybrid covered two and direct covered one. The additional hybrid matches were the menu venue and corroborated missing recipe yield. Both routes matched the invoice total, missed the labeled dish price and failed both labeled rows. On the small clean invoice, direct also retained a separate EUR observation that hybrid missed. Hybrid offers native regions and their coordinates for reviewers.

Three development pages took 383.38 s direct and 589.59 s hybrid end to end. Peak model-process RSS was 7.48 GB direct and 14.03 GB hybrid. Direct is the lower-cost first pass for a workflow that sends every document to review. Use hybrid explicitly when its extra layout evidence justifies the cost. These sparse results give limited evidence for document-specific defaults. The primary and diagnostic held-out cohorts were still sealed when this choice was made.

Both routes use automatic document classification, 2,000,000 render pixels, one retry, one optional crop, and up to 4,096 Qwen output tokens per call. The parser uses at most 32 detected regions for recognition and 768 tokens per crop. The initial 2,048-token runs emitted zero documents on these three sources. The 4,096-token trial also introduced a stronger grammar and recovery of fully closed records at verified token exhaustion. It tests the changes together. The dense menu still reached the larger cap. Labels, normalization, review policy and source images stayed fixed. Synthetic bounds tests retain their published per-case overrides.
