# Handwriting diagnostics

These three English recipe crops were selected specifically for a deletion, a range and a doubtful digit. The sources are two historical manuscripts. Fields were fixed from Wellcome Collection’s scholarly TEI editions and compared with the facsimiles before either diagnostic inference run. The manuscript images carry Wellcome’s Public Domain Mark. The repository includes only the sparse reference facts used in scoring. The original seven-document comparison has separate manifests and denominators.

| Diagnostic | Source and scoring scope |
| --- | --- |
| A Seed Cake | Wellcome Collection, MS.1810, c. 1750, folio marked 51, image 103. The TEI transcription records an active butter quantity near the opening and a deleted butter phrase after the egg yolks. They are separate facts. The labels preserve the transcription’s case-sensitive names “Butter” and “butter” to distinguish the rows. Reporting should distinguish a missed deletion from an abstention on the obscured letters. [Catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/pwcesqe6), [manifest and image rights](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/presentation/b18419665), [transcription](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcome-collection-tei/blob/749ad7ee853ec82917e28349b440215d3175092e/transcribed-manuscripts/MS_1810.xml) |
| To Pot Tongues | Wellcome Collection, Coley family, MS.1711, image 24. The handwritten butter quantity supplies two acceptable amounts, two or three pounds. Both endpoints are scored. The manuscript also contains a deleted spelling of the verb “boil”. Its separate tongue-count alternatives are outside the scoring scope. [Catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/r3hvv23b), [manifest and image rights](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/presentation/b1915723x), [transcription](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcome-collection-tei/blob/749ad7ee853ec82917e28349b440215d3175092e/transcribed-manuscripts/MS_1711_Recipe_Books_Transcript_Trial.xml) |
| A Rich Plumb Cake | Wellcome Collection, Coley family, MS.1711, image 47. The TEI editor recorded a possible half in the sugar quantity with an explicit `unclear` tag. The label requires an ambiguous reading with a null normalized sugar quantity. Sixteen eggs provide a readable control in the same hand. [Catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/r3hvv23b), [manifest and image rights](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/presentation/b1915723x), [transcription](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcome-collection-tei/blob/749ad7ee853ec82917e28349b440215d3175092e/transcribed-manuscripts/MS_1711_Recipe_Books_Transcript_Trial.xml) |

Each input preserves the original handwriting. Its source page, source URL, pixel dimensions, named TEI page, transcription digest and crop-to-page matrix are in `provenance/`. Each source and label digest is also in [the manifest](manifest.json). Crops include complete individual recipes from multi-recipe pages. Small edge traces from neighboring entries can be visible.

The evaluator uses its `held_out` split value and a `diagnostic` stratum for these inputs. This purposive sample can test three concrete failure modes. Case-sensitive name anchors and sparse labels limit the interpretation of exact-match and row scores.
