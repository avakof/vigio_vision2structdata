# Held-out archive fixtures

These four source families were reserved before any Qwen prediction. They provide one English-language example per document family and one French-language handwritten recipe. Each has a small number of labeled fields. Keep their metrics separate from the development cohort. The labels use historical sources and pre-existing human transcriptions. The project has not collected a new blind human annotation panel.

- **Carter receipt**. The Smithsonian describes a handwritten request and receipt for board, with a total of ten dollars. The recto is the test input. Its media record explicitly designates the image CC0. Collection of the Smithsonian National Museum of African American History and Culture, Gift of Liljenquist Family. [Catalogue and rights](https://nmaahc.si.edu/object/nmaahc_2011.104.78), [recto](https://ids.si.edu/ids/deliveryService?id=NMAAHC-2011_104_78_001&max=1600).
- **Delmonico’s menu**. October 7, 1896, page 41831, menu 25680, image 470479. Volunteers transcribed seven dishes in the NYPL database. Four labels use the source's exact dish names and visible price numerals. The dump represents values in dollars. For example, the first dish has the printed numeral 60 and NYPL's value 0.60. The labels avoid assuming an unprinted unit. From The New York Public Library. [Catalogue](https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/510d47db-2b7d-a3d9-e040-e00a18064a99), [image](https://iiif.nypl.org/iiif/2/470479/full/1257,1955/0/default.jpg), [NYPL volunteer dataset, 2021-08-01](https://s3.amazonaws.com/menusdata.nypl.org/gzips/2021_08_01_07_01_17_data.tgz).
- **Bedingfield potato pudding**. Judeth Bedingfield, Ms. Codex 631, volume 1, page 46 [=11v]. Provided by the University of Pennsylvania Libraries. A rectangular crop of the handwritten recipe is the test input. The archive page before cropping and the exact pixel transform are in `provenance/`. We located the recipe using Alyssa Connell's published transcription and its matching facsimile. Fractions and the instruction to sweeten to taste appear in the manuscript. Sugar quantity was never supplied. UPenn marks the manuscript No Copyright – United States. [Catalogue](https://digitalcollections.library.upenn.edu/items/5f8e34b7-c865-4d8e-832e-92b480f3bb15), [IIIF manifest and rights](https://digitalrepository.library.upenn.edu/iiif/items/5f8e34b7-c865-4d8e-832e-92b480f3bb15/manifest), [2015 manuscript transcription](https://rarecooking.com/2015/10/26/to-make-a-potatoe-pudding/).

- **Lutz-Messmer squash recipe**. Marianne Lutz-Messmer, *Koch Buch und sonst allerhand Recept*, 1750, page 153. Zentralbibliothek Zürich, Ms Z VIII 35, Public Domain Mark. The crop includes the German heading and the complete French recipe, including the optional handful of almonds. Yvonne Hausheer transcribed the page as part of the library's citizen-science project. Ingredient names, imprecise quantities and the absence of a stated yield were cross-checked with that transcription. The original scanned page at width 1800 and the crop-to-page transform are in `provenance/`. [Catalogue and rights](https://www.e-manuscripta.ch/zuz/content/titleinfo/644710), [transcription](https://www.e-manuscripta.ch/zuz/content/pagetext/644870), [library's human transcription project](https://www.zb.uzh.ch/de/ueber-uns/citizen-science/zu-tisch-das-kochbuch-und-sonst-allerhand-recept-transkribieren), [IIIF original](https://www.e-manuscripta.ch/i3f/v20/644870/full/1800,/0/default.jpg).

Every image and label file is content-addressed in the evaluation manifest. A missing label for a field means that field was outside the declared scoring scope.
