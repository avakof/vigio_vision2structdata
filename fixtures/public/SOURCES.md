# Public archive evaluation examples

Three genuine documents, each represented by one archive image and a small set of pre-existing human-transcribed facts. Reference labels were selected before local model inference. They are partial labels. They do not establish a human-approved document, complete row recall, calibration, or performance on current commercial documents. All three are development examples. No user documents were supplied.

| Image | Provenance, usage and label scope |
| --- | --- |
| babani-1921.jpg | Babani fashion house invoice, Paris, 1921, inventory 2016.3.1. The total comes from the catalogue description of the purchase, discounts and gratuity were not inferred. The first canvas carries the bill. The museum also catalogues a second image under this accession. CC0, Paris Musées / Palais Galliera. [Museum record and rights](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/palais-galliera/oeuvres/facture), [IIIF manifest](https://apicollections.parismusees.paris.fr/iiif/500103936/manifest). |
| le-versailles-1920-p1.jpg | French restaurant Le Versailles, June 14, 1920, call number 1920-0071_wotm, Buttolph Collection of Menus. From The New York Public Library. First of two pages. The venue and a drink/price pairing come from NYPL’s published example of its 2012 volunteer transcriptions. NYPL lists the transcription as under review. The name label follows that transcription’s spelling and abbreviation. Published in 1920 and in the US public domain. [Item record](https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/bf8b24be-cb02-4876-e040-e00a1806540b), [menu and price references](https://nypl.github.io/menus-api/), [image](https://iiif.nypl.org/iiif/2/4029915/full/1956,2343/0/default.jpg). |
| jefferson-ice-cream.jpg | Thomas Jefferson’s handwritten recipe for ice cream, 1780s. The original manuscript is in the Library of Congress. The 1200 × 4312 recto facsimile is the one published with Monticello’s historical transcription. Labels cover the sugar fraction and unit plus unstated yield in the original manuscript. Use the historical transcription, before the separately published modern adaptation. Manuscript Division, Library of Congress. The catalogue lists no known restrictions on use or reproduction. [Exhibit](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/treasures/tr33b.html#obj36), [item and rights](https://www.loc.gov/resource/mtj1.056_0146_0146/), [Monticello transcription](https://www.monticello.org/jefferson-day/dinner-is-served/ice-creams-were-produced), [image](https://monticello-www.s3.amazonaws.com/files/open-graph/icecream-jefferson.jpg). |

The source text is historical. Preserve layout and spelling when assessing transcription differences. A museum's normalized amount is appropriate for the numeric label. Modernized ingredient amounts from adaptations are excluded. Archive catalogue information that may be absent on the image is omitted from scoring. No model-generated reading is used as a reference. There is no independent held-out real-document cohort in this set.
